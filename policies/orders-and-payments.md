# VoltTech Orders & Payments

## Orders & Payments Section 4.1 - Payment & Receipts (page 1)

VoltTech accepts major credit and debit cards and PayPal at online checkout. Payment is captured when an order is dispatched. A receipt is sent to the email address on the order. If the receipt is missing, check the spam folder and contact support with the order ID to request another copy.

## Orders & Payments Section 4.2 - Order Changes & Cancellations (page 2)

An order can be cancelled or its delivery address changed only before fulfillment begins. Once an order is being packed or has been handed to the carrier, support cannot guarantee a cancellation or address change. Contact support promptly with the order ID; after dispatch, use the carrier's available delivery options.
