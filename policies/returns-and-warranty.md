# VoltTech Returns & Limited Warranty

## Returns & Exchanges Section 2.1 - 30-Day Returns (page 2)

Most VoltTech products may be returned within 30 calendar days of delivery. Opened products are accepted when they are defective or not as described. Include all accessories and the original proof of purchase. Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days.

## Returns & Exchanges Section 2.2 - Return Shipping & Refunds (page 3)

After a return is approved, follow the return instructions provided by support. VoltTech covers return shipping when the item is defective or not as described. For other eligible returns, the customer pays return shipping. Refund processing begins after the returned item is received and inspected; allow 5-7 business days after approval.

## Limited Warranty Section 1.2 - Limited Warranty Coverage (page 4)

Computers and displays include a limited one-year warranty from the delivery date for defects in materials or workmanship. A verified display fault, including flickering during normal use, is covered. Warranty service may result in repair or replacement after inspection.

## Limited Warranty Section 1.4 - Warranty Exclusions (page 5)

Coverage does not apply to accidental damage, liquid ingress, unauthorized repairs or modifications, cosmetic wear that does not affect function, or damage caused by improper installation. If the cause cannot be established from the available information, the item must be inspected before coverage can be confirmed.

## Limited Warranty Section 1.3 - Warranty Claim Process (page 6)

To start a warranty claim, provide the order ID, product serial number, and a description of the issue. Support may request photos or additional information. Do not open or modify the product while a claim is being reviewed. Repair or replacement options are confirmed after inspection.
