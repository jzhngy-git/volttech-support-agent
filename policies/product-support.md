# VoltTech Product Support

## Product Support Section 5.1 - Safe Device Checks (page 1)

For a device that will not power on, check the wall outlet and use the original or manufacturer-approved charger. Disconnect and reconnect the external power cable, wait 30 seconds, then try the normal power button. For display flicker, restart the device and check the external display cable with the device powered off. Do not open the product enclosure. Stop using a device if it is unusually hot, smells unusual, has liquid damage, or has a swollen battery, and contact support. If the basic checks do not resolve the issue, share the order ID and contact support for next steps.
