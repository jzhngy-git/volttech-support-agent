# VoltTech Shipping Policy

## Shipping Policy Section 3.1 - Shipping & Delivery (page 1)

Standard shipping takes 3-7 business days after dispatch. Tracking is updated when the carrier scans a parcel. A parcel marked delivered but not received should be reported within 7 days so the carrier investigation can be opened.

## Shipping Policy Section 3.2 - Delivery Delays & Missing Parcels (page 2)

If tracking has not updated for 2 business days after dispatch, allow one additional business day for the carrier's next scan. If tracking still has no update, contact support with the order ID. Report a parcel marked delivered but not received within 7 days so a carrier investigation can be opened.
